Overview

Jermonie is the in-universe version of the creator within the Planet Blue-Dust universe. He creates characters who inhabit the universe, including characters associated with Starland and the redJSR universe.

Origin

Jermonie and Planet Blue-Dust were created at the same time. They share the same origin within the universe's lore.

Character Creation

Jermonie's role is to create characters. His creations become part of the world and its growing cast of characters. He began creating characters in 1/1/1960.

Role in the Universe

Jermonie is the character creator associated with Planet Blue-Dust. His role is centered on creating characters, rather than traveling between universes.
