Overview

Jcommunist is an 18-year-old alternate-universe version of Jstar. He lives next door to Jstar and has a daily routine that nobody asked for—but he follows it with absolute commitment.

Personality

Jcommunist is a walking shitpost. His humor revolves around absurd Soviet and Russian jokes, random music, and his unstoppable need to yap about Russian food. These jokes are absurd humor and do not represent an actual political ideology.

Daily Routine

At exactly 5:30 PM, Jcommunist arrives at Jstar's house to talk about Russian food. This is apparently a scheduled appointment that only he knows about.

Entrance

Jcommunist does not knock. Instead, he drives straight through Jstar's wall while blasting the Soviet anthem. The wall is simply part of the arrival process.

Music

At night, Jcommunist blasts music by Parry Gripp, because apparently the Soviet anthem isn't enough noise for one day.

Favorite Food

Waffles. Jcommunist is obsessed with them. Despite his constant Russian-food discussions, waffles remain his favorite food.

Catchphrase

“What's up, Comrade?”

Relationships

  • Jstar: His neighbor, frequent visitor, and the unfortunate owner of the wall Jcommunist drives through.
