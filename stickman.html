Overview

Stickman is one of the main characters in The Incredible Adventures of Jstar (TIAOJ), also known as redJSR TIAOJ. He is an 18-year-old male stick figure who stands six feet tall. He is one of Jstar's closest friends and lives with Jstar, Simariki and Junior in Starland on Planet Blue-Dust.

Appearance

Stickman is depicted as a stick figure. He is six feet tall. Further details about his current design and clothing can be shown in the character images above.

Personality

Stickman has a goofy, carefree personality. His behavior has been compared to Patrick Star from SpongeBob SquarePants. He is generally portrayed as the laid-back member of his friend group, contrasting with Jstar's more easily frustrated temperament.

Occupation

Stickman does not have a job. Unlike Jstar, who works at Spaceraids, Stickman spends his time outside of employment and lives with his friends.

History

Stickman met Jstar when they were in fifth grade. The two became longtime friends and eventually lived together in Starland. Stickman also shares a home with Simariki and Jstar's younger brother, Junior.

Relationships

  • Jstar: Stickman's longtime friend. They met in fifth grade and live together.
  • Simariki: Stickman's housemate, a 17-year-old squirrel inventor.
  • Junior: Jstar's younger brother, who also lives in the household.
