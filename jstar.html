Jstar

Category: Main character

Description

Jstar Berrison is an 18-year-old red alien. He wears a dark-blue shirt with a white star and has antennae that turn blue when he is sad. He works at Spaceraids in downtown Starland and is known there as the “Squidward of Spaceraids.”

Relationships

Stickman, Simariki, and Junior live with Jstar.
