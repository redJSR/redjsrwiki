About the creator

Welcome to redJSR Wiki

The Incredible Adventures of Jstar (TIAOJ), also known as redJSR TIAOJ, is a comic series about Jstar and the characters around him.

This wiki collects information about the series and its characters. The series is currently a comic; episodes have not been released.
