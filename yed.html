Overview

Yed is a nine-year-old male character in the redJSR TIAOJ universe. He is Jstar Berrison's cousin and belongs to the extended Berrison family. Yed is yellow and has wings, making him visually distinct from his red alien cousin.

Appearance

Yed is yellow and has wings. His winged design is one of his defining physical features. His current appearance and first appearance are shown in the images above.

Family

Yed is Jstar's cousin and has four siblings. Through his family connection to Jstar, he is also connected to the wider Berrison family, including Jstar's younger siblings, Junior and Dream.

Relationships

  • Jstar Berrison: Yed's cousin, an eighteen-year-old red alien.
  • Junior Berrison: Jstar's younger brother and a member of the extended family.
  • Dream Berrison: Jstar's younger sister and a member of the extended family.
